الطريقة الأولى — من جوه اللعبة (فوري)

  1. افتح اللعبة وروح للشاشة الرئيسية.
  2. انزل لآخر الشاشة ودوس «🗑 حذف حسابي وبياناتي».
  3. أكّد الحذف — حسابك وبياناتك بيتمسحوا فورًا ونهائيًا.

ابعت طلب حذف من إيميلك على:

noura7med321@gmail.com

اكتب اسم اللاعب بتاعك في الرسالة، وهننفذ الحذف في أقرب وقت (خلال أيام قليلة كحد أقصى).

Delete your account and data — Heart Online ♥

App: Heart Online · Developer: Nour Ahmed

Option 1 — Inside the game (instant)

  1. Open the game and go to the home screen.
  2. Scroll down and tap "🗑 Delete my account and data".
  3. Confirm — your account and data are deleted immediately and permanently.

Option 2 — By email

Send a deletion request to:

noura7med321@gmail.com

Include your player name in the message; we will complete the deletion within a few days at most.

What gets deleted?

All of your account data: name and avatar, XP and level, coins, and your friends list (your name is also removed from other players' friend lists). Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. In-room chat messages disappear automatically when the room ends.
